# Brand Sting / Recurring Intro

**What it is:** A short, repeatable piece that plays identically every time it's used — a logo animating in at the end of other videos, a show's recurring open, an app's launch animation. Includes what's sometimes called a "title sequence" when it's a broadcast-style recurring intro rather than a one-off.

**When to use this category:** The request is for something under ~8 seconds that will be reused as-is many times, not watched once. "Make our logo animate in," "I need an intro for my YouTube videos," "animate our app's splash screen."

**Length:** 2–6s typically; a recurring broadcast intro can run to 8–10s. Never longer — if it needs more time to land, it's not a sting anymore.

## How to build it

1. **Identify the one gesture.** A sting has exactly one motion idea: a shape resolving into the mark, the mark itself drawing on, or a single camera move revealing it. Do not combine two gestures (e.g. a shape morph *and* a camera push) — pick one.
2. **Design for repetition, not novelty.** The viewer will see this dozens of times. Favor a clean, confident, slightly understated move over something flashy that would grate on the 20th viewing. This is the opposite instinct from a one-off social clip.
3. **Timing:** use the full ~1–2s for the gesture itself, then hold the resolved mark for at least 0.5–1s before cutting — a sting that resolves and immediately cuts away reads as unfinished.
4. **Sound:** a single, deliberate SFX hit on the resolve beat is standard for this category (see `../motion-rules.md` for gain/peak-placement defaults) — this is one of the few categories where a sound hit is almost always warranted, unlike most scenes in longer videos.
5. **Loop-safety:** if this will be used as a loop or played back-to-back with itself, confirm the first and last frame don't create a visible jump — check this explicitly in validation (Section 4 of the main skill), since it's specific to this category.

**Pitfalls specific to this category:**
- Treating it like a mini social promo (multiple beats, a CTA) — a sting has no CTA and no narrative arc, just the mark.
- Making it too long because "more time to show off the animation" — length discipline matters more here than almost any other category, since it plays before/after content the viewer actually wants.
- A gesture that only works once (e.g. relies on surprise) — it needs to still read well on the 50th play.

**Transitions:** A sting is usually one continuous gesture with no internal cut at all — if it has two beats (e.g. a shape resolving, then the wordmark appearing), use a match cut where the shape itself becomes part of the wordmark rather than cutting away from it. See `../transitions.md`.

**Expected output bar:** Identical, clean playback every time; the mark reads instantly even to someone who's seen it many times before.
