# Design System

A unified brand guide establishing colors, typography, voice, and graphics for consistent communication and visual identity across interfaces, documents, presentations, and media assets.

---

## 1. Color Palette

### Brand Palette (Canvas, Surfaces & Text)

| Token | Hex Value | Role & Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Dominant canvas** | `#08090a` | Primary background canvas; deep, near-black grounding surface. |
| **Inset surface** | `#0f1011` | Inset panels, cards, containers, secondary layers. |
| **Primary text** | `#f7f8f8` | High-contrast body, primary titles, crisp legible copy. |
| **Quiet text** | `#8a8f98` | Secondary labels, muted captions, subdued metadata. |
| **Supporting text** | `#d0d6e0` | Intermediate explanatory copy, subtitles, descriptive blocks. |
| **Compact highlight** | `#e5e5e6` | High-emphasis compact text, active chips, badges, focus cues. |

### Graphics Palette

Build graphics from crisp geometric forms, rounded linework, and restrained charcoal, slate, and indigo against white or deep navy.

| Token | Hex Value | Role & Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Charcoal** | `#2a2d33` | Structural linework, geometric borders, deep neutral fills. |
| **Slate** | `#5c6370` | Secondary linework, intermediate shapes, balancing neutrals. |
| **Indigo accent** | `#4f46e5` | Key focal points, primary action accents, spotlight symbols (use sparingly). |

## 2. Typography

### Core Typeface: Inter

We use **Inter** exclusively as the core typeface, with restrained weight changes and clear scale shifts to create an orderly, modern hierarchy.

- Set key messages in a large medium weight, then use smaller headlines and concise supporting copy to guide readers through presentations, documents, and social designs.
- Keep body text regular and calm; use size and placement, rather than decorative styling, to distinguish levels of information.

### Weight Hierarchy

| Weight | Numeric Value | Usage Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Regular** | `400` | Body copy and supporting explanations. |
| **Medium** | `500` | Primary cover statements, section headlines, compact labels. |
| **Semibold** | `600` | Short high-priority details only. |

### Typography Do's & Don'ts

#### Do
- ✓ **Use Inter Medium** for cover statements and other primary messages, then repeat it at a smaller scale for section headlines.
- ✓ **Set supporting explanations** in regular Inter, reserving larger supporting text for concise, high-priority details.
- ✓ **Use modest weight emphasis** for short labels or names within otherwise regular copy.

#### Don't
- × **Avoid giving long paragraphs** the same large, medium-weight treatment as a key message; it erases the observed distinction between headline and supporting copy.
- × **Do not introduce** a contrasting display typeface or ornate letter styling that competes with Inter’s restrained, minimal typographic character.

## 3. Brand Voice

### Character & Tone

Our brand voice is **clear, thoughtful, and confident**. We use concise, human language that turns complex technical ideas into simple, practical solutions. We speak with the confidence of builders, focusing on real problems, useful products, and measurable outcomes rather than technical jargon. The tone is curious and forward-thinking, balancing technical precision with an approachable, problem-solving mindset.

### Communication Summary

Keep communication clear and purposeful:
- Lead with the core idea.
- Highlight its practical value.
- Frame technology around better ways to solve real problems.
- Balance confident thinking with concrete, product-focused detail, making complex ideas feel simple, useful, and actionable.

### Voice Do's & Don'ts

#### Do
- ✓ **Use short, clear headlines** that communicate a strong idea, capability, or point of view.
- ✓ **Lead with the problem and outcome**, showing what is being solved and why it matters.
- ✓ **Use concrete, action-oriented language** such as *build*, *solve*, *simplify*, *automate*, *improve*, and *scale*.
- ✓ **Use technical and product vocabulary** where it adds precision, while keeping complex ideas simple and understandable.
- ✓ **Frame technology and AI around real-world value**, focusing on better workflows, less friction, useful products, and meaningful results.

#### Don't
- × **Avoid exaggerated claims**, buzzwords, or unsupported promises; keep confidence grounded in real capabilities and outcomes.
- × **Don’t make the brand sound overly corporate**, salesy, or self-important; keep the language human and approachable.
- × **Avoid unnecessary technical jargon** or complex explanations that make simple ideas harder to understand.
- × **Don’t focus on technology for its own sake**; always connect tools, features, and AI to a real problem or practical benefit.
- × **Avoid vague, lengthy, or overly abstract language**; keep communication concise, purposeful, and easy to act on.

## 4. Graphics & Iconography Guides

Build graphics from crisp geometric forms, rounded linework, and restrained charcoal, slate, and indigo against white or deep navy.

### Graphics Do's & Don'ts

#### Do
- ✓ **Use solid, simple silhouettes** with ample negative space.
- ✓ **Draw icons** with consistent thick strokes and softly rounded ends.
- ✓ **Use indigo sparingly** (`#4f46e5`) to spotlight a key symbol.
- ✓ **Keep dimensional objects** in restrained isometric views with fine edge definition.

#### Don't
- × **Avoid gradients**, photographic lighting, and expressive painterly texture.
- × **Do not mix** thin delicate strokes with the icons’ bold, rounded linework.
- × **Avoid bright multicolor palettes** or decorative details that compete with the central form.

## 5. Quality Checklist

Before finalizing any design, UI component, copy, or visual asset:

- [ ] **Canvas & Surfaces:** Uses `#08090a` for canvas and `#0f1011` for inset surfaces.
- [ ] **Text Contrast:** Body copy uses `#f7f8f8`, supporting copy uses `#d0d6e0`, and subdued details use `#8a8f98`.
- [ ] **Typography:** Uses Inter exclusively (weights 400, 500, 600) with proper scale shifts.
- [ ] **Tone & Copy:** Grounded, concise, problem-and-outcome-focused; free of hype and empty buzzwords.
- [ ] **Graphics:** Geometric silhouettes, rounded thick linework, restrained color palette, and minimal indigo accents.
